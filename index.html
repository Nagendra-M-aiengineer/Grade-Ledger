How is SGPA calculated?

SGPA = Σ(Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ(Credit) — every subject's grade point is weighted by its credit value, summed, then divided by the total credits for the semester.

Example: a 4-credit subject graded A (9 points) contributes 36 quality points; a 3-credit subject graded B+ (7 points) contributes 21. SGPA = (36 + 21) ÷ (4 + 3) = 8.14.

How is CGPA calculated?

CGPA = Σ(Semester Credits × Semester SGPA) ÷ Σ(Semester Credits) — each semester's SGPA is weighted by that semester's total credits across your whole program so far.

Example: Semester 1 — 20 credits at 8.00 SGPA; Semester 2 — 22 credits at 8.50 SGPA. CGPA = (20×8.00 + 22×8.50) ÷ (20+22) = 8.26.

How the numbers work

SGPA — Semester Grade Point Average

Multiply each subject's credit by its grade point, add those up, then divide by the total credits for that semester. It measures performance in a single term.

CGPA — Cumulative Grade Point Average

The same idea applied across every semester: each semester's SGPA is weighted by its credit total, summed across all semesters, then divided by the total credits earned so far.

Percentage equivalent & vice versa

Most universities publish a formula to translate between CGPA and percentage (such as CGPA × 10, CGPA × 9.5, or [CGPA − 0.75] × 10). This calculator works in both directions: CGPA/SGPA to percentage, and percentage to equivalent CGPA.
